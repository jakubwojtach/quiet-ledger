Holdings and cashflow, quietly

The same composition as the app: savings, installments, and live comps when you turn them on — all valued locally in PLN.

  • Holdings ETFs, funds, cash, property, cars — paid vs worth.
  • Cashflow Income, expenses, and loan installments by month.
  • Comps Otodom for flats & land, Otomoto for cars.

Your machine is the server

SQLite on disk. Optional portal logins in OS secure storage. Nothing secret ships in the installer.
