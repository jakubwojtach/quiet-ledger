Quiet Ledger

Local-first net worth and cashflow. Base currency PLN. No cloud account.

Download View source

macOS today · Windows via CI releases

One composition for your money

Savings holdings, installments, income and expenses — valued locally, with live market comps when you want them.

  • Holdings ETFs, funds, cash, property, cars — paid vs worth in PLN.
  • Cashflow Income, expenses, and loan installments in one monthly view.
  • Comps Otodom for flats & land, Otomoto for cars — median of similar listings.

Your machine is the server

SQLite on disk. Optional portal logins in OS secure storage. Nothing secret ships in the installer.
